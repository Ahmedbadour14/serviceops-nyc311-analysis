Know what the numbers mean

01 / GRAIN

One request, one row

73,362 unique service-request IDs. A request is not a unique citizen or necessarily a distinct real-world incident.

02 / DENOMINATOR

72,991 valid durations

73,005 records have Closed status; 14 lack a valid closure timestamp. Missing durations are excluded, never zero-filled.

03 / INTERPRETATION

Closed ≠ resolved

Closure is an agency record status. It does not prove the problem was fixed or the citizen was satisfied.

04 / TIME

A historical cohort, not history

Created Jan–Mar 2025, status extracted Nov 23, 2026. The 357 non-closed records are not the March 2025 backlog.

Limits, provenance and source

NYC timestamps are local floating time; DST can affect durations by an hour. Closed-case durations have censoring/selection bias. Due date is an expected agency update, not a universal resolution SLA. This portfolio project is not an official agency assessment. Full definitions, authorship and learning exercises are in the accompanying project files.
